## iPhone

You will receive a TestFlight invitation by email from Apple.

1. On your iPhone, install **TestFlight** from the App Store if you do not already have it.

<img src="/Users/wang/Downloads/IMG_0255.jpg" alt="IMG_0255" style="zoom:25%;" />

2. Open the invitation email on your iPhone and tap **View in TestFlight**.

<img src="/Users/wang/Downloads/IMG_0250 3.jpg" alt="IMG_0250 3" style="zoom:30%;" />

3. In TestFlight, tap **Accept**, then **Install** next to ELLA.

<img src="/Users/wang/Downloads/IMG_0251.jpg" alt="IMG_0251" style="zoom:35%;" />







## Android

The ELLA team will send you a download link for the Android installation file.

1. Open the link on your Android phone and download the **ELLA APK** file (**ella-release.apk**).
2. Open the downloaded file. If your phone blocks the installation, follow its on-screen instructions to allow it. For example, if you see the warning shown below, tap **Install anyway** to continue.
